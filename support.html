Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Change Order AI and tap Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID.

The AI could not extract line items.

Check your internet connection and try again. If you are offline, the app saves your recording and photos on the device so you can retry later or add line items manually.

Where is my data stored?

Your projects and documents are stored locally on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I edit a signed change order?

No. A signed change order is locked so the signed record cannot change. You can void it or create a new change order.
