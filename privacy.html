Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Change Order AI: Estimate PDF ("the app") helps contractors turn voice memos and job-site photos into change order PDFs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

Projects, change orders, line items, signatures, photos, voice recordings, client contact details you enter, price lists and generated PDFs are stored locally on your device. We do not run an account system and we do not upload this data to our own servers for storage.

Data sent to third parties

  • AI processing (Google Gemini API): when you tap "Generate Change Order with AI", the voice memo, up to 3 site photos, your text notes, selected quick tags, your default hourly rate and your saved price list are sent for processing by Google's Gemini API in order to extract line items and draft the scope of work. The request may pass through a relay server operated for this app that forwards it to Google and applies daily usage limits; the relay does not store the content of your requests. Do not include information you are not comfortable sending. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat): purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status to manage your Pro entitlement. We do not receive your payment details.

Data you share

When you export or share a PDF (for example by iMessage, WhatsApp or email), or use "Email to Client" / "Text to Client", the PDF and message are sent to the recipient you choose through iOS. We do not see or store them.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record field voice memos.
  • Camera and Photos: to attach job-site photos as evidence.

No tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not contain advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete a draft or delete the app. Signed change orders cannot be edited or deleted inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Data already sent to Google or to recipients of shared PDFs is outside our control.

Children

The app is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
